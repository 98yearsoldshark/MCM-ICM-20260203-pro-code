# 1.1 Problem Background

随着移动智能终端的深度普及，智能手机已从简单的通信工具演变为集办公、娱乐、社交及地理定位于一体的微型计算中心 。然而，这种功能的多样化也对能源管理提出了严峻挑战，用户常面临电量消耗“不可预测”的困扰：即便在相似的活跃时长下，手机电量有时能支撑一整天，有时却在午餐前告急。电池续航能力往往成为制约用户体验的瓶颈 。

With the widespread adoption of mobile smart devices, smartphones have evolved fromsimple communication tools into miniature computing hubs that integrate office functions,entertainment, social networking, and geolocation services. However, this functional diversityposes significant challenges for energy management, leaving users frequently frustrated byunpredictable battery consumption: even with similar usage patterns, a phone's battery mightlast all day one time, yet run low before lunchtime another. Battery endurance has become abottleneck that constrains the user experience.

The consumption of mobile phone battery power is a dynamic process involving multipleintertwined factors. In practical use, the battery discharge rate is not constant; it is influenced bythe phone's internal multi-dimensional hardware load, software scheduling, and backgroundresident tasks. Additionally, mobile phones commonly use lithium-ion batteries, which areextremely sensitive to temperature. In low-temperature environments, increased internalresistance reduces the battery's usable capacity. Conversely, under high temperatures orsustained heavy loads, the battery may trigger frequency-limiting protection due tooverheating. Furthermore, as charge-discharge cycles accumulate, irreversible degradationoccurs within the battery's internal materials, gradually diminishing its maximum capacity. Thisaging characteristic makes predicting "remaining battery life" increasingly challenging.

# 7 Sensitivity Analysis

模型的分析 ：在建模比赛中模型分析主要有两种，一个是灵敏度(性)分析，另一个是误差分析。灵敏度分析是研究与分析一个系统（或模型）的状态或输出变化对系统参数或周围条件变化的敏感程度的方法。其通用的步骤是：控制其他参数不变的情

况下，改变模型中某个重要参数的值，然后观察模型的结果的变化情况。误差分析是指分析模型中的误差来源，或者估算模型中存在的误差，一般用于预测问题或者数值计算类问题。

模型的检验：模型检验可以分为两种，一种是使用模型之前应该进行的检验，例如层次分析法中一致性检验，灰色预测中的准指数规律的检验，这部分内容应该放在模型的建立部分；另一种是使用了模型后对模型的结果进行检验，数模中最常见的是稳定性检验，实际上这里的稳定性检验和前面的灵敏度分析非常类似，等会大家看到例子就明白了。

在美赛的写作中，写的最多的就是灵敏度分析（Sensitivity Analysis），因此这里我们的标题就直接取得是灵敏度分析；如果你既要写灵敏度分析，又要写误差分析（Error Analysis），那么你可以把标题改成： Sensitivity Analysis and Error Analysis
