### 2. 6.4 Numerical Stability Analysis
To eliminate the interference of "numerical discretization errors" on the conclusions, we conducted a step-size sensitivity test. We repeated the simulations using both the baseline step size \( \Delta t = 10 \, \text{s} \) (used in the main experiments for efficiency) and the high-precision step size \( \Delta t = 1 \, \text{s} \). Define the numerical relative deviation:
$$
\varepsilon_{\text{num}} = \left| \frac{\text{TTE}_{\Delta t=1} - \text{TTE}_{\Delta t=10}}{\text{TTE}_{\Delta t=1}} \right| \times 100\%
$$

Test results indicate that under all test scenarios, \( \varepsilon_{\text{num}} \) remains below **0.01%** (maximum observed \( \varepsilon_{\text{num}} \approx 0.0040\% \)). This demonstrates that the continuous-time ODE solver we employed exhibits excellent convergence. The model results accurately reflect physical laws rather than numerical artifacts.

> 注：为把“ODE 数值离散误差”与“随机过程离散化误差”区分开，我们在该测试中关闭了 Poisson/Markov 等随机机制（只保留确定性 schedule），从而更纯粹地检验 ODE 积分步长对结果的影响。主实验仍使用 `dt=10s`（效率更高）。


### 3. 6.3 Input Perturbation: Using Pattern Fluctuation Analysis
Real-world user operations are not perfectly steady-state and are often accompanied by random fluctuations. To test the model's immunity to input noise, we superimposed dynamic noise onto brightness \( b(t) \), data throughput \( q(t) \), and CPU load \( u(t) \):
$$
b_{\text{noise}}(t) = \text{clip}(b(t)(1 + \delta_{\text{b}}), 0, 1), \quad \delta_{\text{b}} \sim \text{U}[-0.1, 0.1]
$$
$$
q_{\text{noise}}(t) = q(t)(1 + \delta_{\text{q}}), \quad \delta_{\text{q}} \sim \text{U}[-0.2, 0.2]
$$
$$
u_{\text{noise}}(t) = \text{clip}(u_{\text{cpu}}(t)(1 + \delta_{\text{u}}), 0, 1), \quad \delta_{\text{u}} \sim \text{U}[-0.15, 0.15]
$$

We ran the SOC-thermal coupling model with the aforementioned noise injection for 50 iterations (per scenario). Results show that despite the instantaneous power \( P_{\text{device}}(t) \) fluctuating dramatically with input, the final output's TTE relative deviation consistently remained within **<0.6%** (maximum observed ≈ **0.51%** across tested scenarios). This exceptional stability stems from the battery's integrative characteristics. SOC evolution follows the integral equation:
$$
\text{SOC}(t) = \text{SOC}(0) - \frac{1}{Q_{\text{act}}} \int_{0}^{t} I(\tau) d\tau
$$

> 注：上述噪声为“随时间动态注入”（每个积分步独立抽样），因此其均值接近 0，主要影响短时功率而非长期平均功率；同时我们关闭了其他 Poisson/Markov 随机机制，以避免把“使用随机过程”与“输入扰动”混在一起（两者在 Q3 的主实验中会分别讨论）。
