The consumption of mobile phone battery power is a dynamic process involving multipleintertwined factors. In practical use, the battery discharge rate is not constant; it is influencedby the phone's internal multi-dimensional hardware load, software scheduling, andbackground resident tasks. Additionally, mobile phones commonly use lithium-ion batteries,which are extremely sensitive to temperature. In low-temperature environments, increasedinternal resistance reduces the battery's usable capacity. Conversely, under hightemperatures or sustained heavy loads, the battery may trigger frequency-limiting protectiondue to overheating. Furthermore, as charge-discharge cycles accumulate, irreversibledegradation occurs within the battery's internal materials, gradually diminishing its maximumcapacity. This aging characteristic makes predicting "remaining battery life" increasinglychallenging.

# 2. Assumptions and Justifications

1.

Assumption 1. Device and Physical Homogeneity (设备与物理同质性)

所有建模设备均遵循通用的锂离子电池电化学动力学与 CMOS 功耗定律，设备间差异仅通过参数调整体现，而非模型结构改变。

Justification: 现代智能手机普遍采用基于 Nernst 方程的 Li-ion 电池及遵循 $P \proptoCV^2f$ 的处理器。物理机理的普适性使得通用的机理模型（Mechanism Model）能够适配不同品牌设备，无需针对特定硬件重构控制方程。

# Assumption 2. Quasi-Stationary User Behavior (准稳态用户行为)

用户操作在短时间内具有时间局部性（Temporal Locality），模型将连续负载处理为分段稳态过程。

Justification: 用户行为（如观看视频、游戏）通常具有惯性。将复杂动态过程划分为具有平稳特征的时间片，允许我们使用分段函数近似负载波动，在不损失统计显著性的前提下大幅降低计算复杂度。

# Assumption 3. Thermodynamic Simplification (热力学简化)

电池的热耦合系数 η为常数，且忽略环境温度的剧烈波动，假设设备运行在典型室温条件下。

Justification: 尽管热对流是一个复杂的多物理场过程，但在典型室内工况下，电池与环境的温差变化范围有限。根据文献 [1]，将其简化为线性传热模型（常数系数）足以捕捉主要的热-电耦合效应，避免了求解高维流体动力学方程的过拟合风险。

# Assumption 4. Discharge Cycle Isolation (放电循环隔离)

放电过程被严格独立划分，数据集中不包含充电或非物理的 SOC 跳变。

Justification: 充电（能量注入）与放电（能量耗散）遵循截然不同的电化学机制且不可逆。混合数据会破坏能量守恒方程的单调性。严格的循环分割确保了模型积分区间的物理封闭性与数学严密性。

# 5. Model 1：Continuous-time model for battery stateof charge

# 5.1 The Establishment of Model 1

Based on a physical abstraction of smartphone power supply systems, this study addresses

the limitation of traditional discrete fitting models in explaining "unpredictable powerdepletion" by treating smartphones as multi-field coupled open thermodynamic systems. Weconstruct a Hybrid Dynamical System that deeply couples the battery's electrochemicalreactions and thermodynamic evolution with discrete events at the upper system layer (userbehavior, component state machines). Unlike conventional power consumption superpositionmodels, this approach precisely captures the "synergistic effects" and "unpredictability"during battery depletion by integrating a finite state machine (FSM) power layer withcontinuous-time differential equations.

# 2. Weaknesses（局限）

参数先验为区间型（Uniform），缺少真实数据拟合，区间更多反映“可行范围”而非严格统计置信。

1. 芯片 电池热耦合用等效项表示，未显式建模多节点热阻网络。

1. 网络功耗未区分上下行与不同调制/功率控制细节，弱信号惩罚为简化代理。

1. 当接近 $\Delta { \approx } 0$ 边界时，求解对噪声与步长更敏感，需要更谨慎的数值处理。

# 3. 可改进路径

1. 优先采集/标定：hA、τ_m、b_{q,m}（它们往往主导区间与风险）；其次是 k_gpu、k_big。

1. 若有温度/外壳数据：拟合η(T) 或 R0(T) 的更准确形式；若有网络层日志：细化弱信号惩罚与上下行差异。

1. 数值改进：对 $\Delta$ 接近 0 的时刻采用更小步长与稳健迭代（或直接以 $\Delta$ 作为事件函数）。
