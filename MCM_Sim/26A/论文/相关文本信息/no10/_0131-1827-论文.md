# 1 Introduction

# 1.1 Problem Background

With the widespread adoption of mobile smart devices, smartphones have evolved fromsimple communication tools into miniature computing hubs that integrate office functions,entertainment, social networking, and geolocation services. However, this functional diversityposes significant challenges for energy management, Battery endurance has become abottleneck that constrains the user experience.

Figure 1: Phone battery level


The consumption of mobile phone battery power is a dynamic process involving multipleintertwined factors. In practical use, the battery discharge rate is not constant; it is influenced bythe phone's internal multi-dimensional hardware load, software scheduling, and backgroundresident tasks. Additionally, mobile phones commonly use lithium-ion batteries, which areextremely sensitive to. In low-temperature environments, increased internal resistance reducesthe battery's usable capacity. Conversely, under high temperatures or sustained heavy loads, thebattery may trigger frequency-limiting protection due to overheating. Furthermore, as charge-discharge cycles accumulate, irreversible degradation occurs within the battery's internalmaterials, gradually diminishing its maximum capacity. This aging characteristic makespredicting "remaining battery life" increasingly challenging.

This necessitatesimprovements to our foundational models.

# 2 Assumptions and Justifications

In order to simplify the problem and improve the model's tractability and computationalefficiency, we make the following assumptions

Assumption 1: Assuming the use of lithium-ion batteries.

Justifications 1: Lithium-ion batteries represent the mainstream power solution in thesmartphone sector, offering significant advantages in high energy density and longcycle life. Establishing a unified chemical framework facilitates standardizedmodeling using known electrochemical parameters.

 Assumption 2: Assumes the battery state changes continuously over time.

Justifications 2: Battery charging and discharging constitute a macroscopicelectrochemical reaction process without abrupt jumps. Based on this assumption, wecan employ Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) to precisely describe thetrajectory of the battery state's dynamic evolution over time.

Assumption 3: Battery capacity gradually degrades with increasing charge-dischargecycles.

Justifications 3: This inherent physical characteristic of lithium-ion batteries enablesthe model to reflect not only short-term capacity changes but also assess long-term

device health.

Assumption 4: Transitions between high and low operating states are calculated asinfinitesimally small time intervals.

Justifications 4: Although microsecond-level physical delays exist in hardware circuitvoltage and current stabilization, these transition times are negligible relative to thebattery's discharge cycles spanning several hours, allowing for simplified modelanalysis.

假设1：假设使用锂离子电池，

解释 1：锂离子电池是目前智能手机领域的主流电源方案，具备高能量密度和长循环寿命的显著优势。设定统一的化学背景有助于我们利用已知的电化学参数进行标准化建模。

假设 2：假设电池的状态随时间呈连续变化

解释 2：电池的充放电是一个宏观的电化学反应过程，不具备突发性跳转。基于此假设，我们可以运用微分方程（Ordinary Differential Equations）来精确描述电池状态随时间动态演变的轨迹。

假设3：假设电池容量随充放电循环次数的增加而逐渐衰减

解释3：这是锂离子电池固有的物理特性，这样使模型不仅能反映“短期使用”的电量变化，还能评估设备的“长期健康度”

假设4：高工作和低工作状态之间的转化按时间无限小计算

# 4.1.1 Basic Differential Equation Derivation

First, we define the battery State of Charge (SOC) as the percentage of the currentremaining charge relative to the rated capacity:

$$
S O C (t) = \frac {Q (t)}{Q _ {\mathrm {eff}} (t)} \times 100 \%
$$

Where:

Q(t) is the remaining charge at time t(unit: mAh).

$Q _ { \mathrm { e f f } } ( t )$ is the total battery capacity (unit: mAh).

At ${ \mathrm { S O C } } = 1$ , the battery is fully charged; at ${ \mathrm { S O C } } = 0$ , the system considers the battery"depleted".

At the same time, lithium-ion batteries have an inherent characteristic: their capacitygradually degrades with increasing number of charge cycles. Therefore, when designing thebattery model, we also factor in this characteristic.

$$
C _ {t} h \frac {d T}{d t} = I (t) ^ {2} R 0 (S O C, T, S O H) + \alpha_ {c} h i p P _ {c} h i p (t) - h A (T - T _ {a} m b (t))
$$

$$
V _ {\text {t e r m}} (t) = O C V \left(S O C, T\right) - I \cdot R 0 \left(S O C, T, S O H\right) - U 1 - U 2
$$

We define the effective available capacity of the battery $Q _ { \mathrm { e f f } } ( t )$ as a function of state ofcharge, expressed as follows:

$$
Q _ {\text {e f f}} (t) = Q _ {0} \cdot f _ {T} (T (t)) \cdot f _ {C} (I (t)) \cdot f _ {A} (A)
$$

As shown, the effective capacity is highly dependent on the nominal capacity of a newbattery under standard conditions, the temperature correction function , the battery or celltemperature , the discharge rate/current impact function , the aging (SOH) impact function , andaging indicators such as cycle count .

Substituting the definition of SOC into the above equation yields the rate of change ofSOC:

$$
\frac {d (S O C (t))}{d t} = \frac {1}{Q _ {\mathrm {eff}} (t)} \times \frac {d Q (t)}{d t} \times 100 \% = \frac {- I (t)}{Q _ {\mathrm {eff}} (t)} \times 100 \%
$$

$I ( t ) { = } \frac { P _ { \mathrm { s y s } } ( t ) } { \eta ( t ) \ V _ { \mathrm { b a t } } ( t ) } \mathrm { T o }$ simplify the model, using mAh/h as the power consumption unitallows $I ( t )$ to be converted into the power P we wish to study. Therefore, the finalfundamental differential equation is:

$$
\frac {d S O C}{d t} = \frac {- P}{Q _ {\mathrm {eff}} (t)} \times 100 \%
$$

This equation describes the relationship between the rate of SOC change over time andpower consumption: as power consumption increases, the SOC decline accelerates; as batterycapacity increases, the SOC decline slows down.

# 4.2.1 Discharge cut-off voltage setting

First, considering that in reality, the cut-off voltage of lithium-ion batteries significantlyimpacts factors such as "available capacity" and "lifespan/safety margin," this paper assumes aconservative strategy aligned with smartphone systems (avoiding deep discharge to preventlifespan degradation and shutdown uncertainty): Vcut $= 3 . 3$

Simultaneously, sensitivity analysis will perturb Vcut as a key parameter (e.g., 3.2–3.4 V)to evaluate its impact on TTE and undervoltage probability, thereby explicitly revealing thetrade-off between "cut-off voltage selection—endurance—lifespan."

In real-world smartphone usage, prolonged operation can lead to overheating and rapidbattery drain, while low outdoor temperatures may also cause accelerated power loss. While abattery's energy storage capacity is fixed, its "available energy" is dynamically constrained byenvironmental factors and its own thermal state.

For instance, in high-temperature scenarios (such as running demanding games), intenseheat generated by the SoC chip is conducted through the device body to the battery, causing itstemperature $R _ { 0 }$ to rise. While this short-term heating slightly reduces the internal resistance$R _ { 0 }$ , the overall internal resistance still increases, generating more heat. Consequently, the power$P _ { b a t t }$ required to maintain the same functionality is forced to rise. Conversely, in low-temperature environments (e.g., cold outdoor conditions), $R _ { 0 }$ increases exponentially. Tomaintain the same output power under energy conservation, the battery must deliver asignificantly higher current I . This substantial current not only accelerates SOC depletion butalso causes the terminal voltage $V _ { t e r m }$ to prematurely reach the critical $V _ { c u t }$ threshold due to thesharp increase in the voltage drop $I ^ { . } R _ { 0 }$ . This results in the so-called "sudden power drop"phenomenon.

To unify these complex physical variables within an energy balance framework, weestablish the following battery-side current solution model:

We define the battery's effective open-circuit voltage $V _ { o c }$ . This is not simply the open-circuit voltage OCV , but rather the result after subtracting the voltage drops $U _ { 1 }$ $U _ { 1 } { \mathrm { a n d } } U _ { 2 }$ causedby polarization effects. This reflects the battery's "fatigue effect"—after operating at highcurrent for a period, the usable voltage temporarily decreases. Terminal voltage $V _ { t e r m }$ : This isthe voltage actually sensed by the phone's circuitry. When this value drops to your set cutoffvoltageV cut (3.3V), the system will force a shutdown.

Phone components (screen, CPU, 5G module, etc.) require power to operate.

$$
V _ {o c} = O C V (S O C, T) - U 1 - U 2, V _ {t e r m} = V _ {o c} - I \cdot R _ {0}
$$

$$
P _ {b a t t} = V _ {t e r m} \cdot I = V _ {o c} I - R _ {0} I ^ {2}
$$

The $R _ { 0 } I ^ { 2 }$ term represents internal resistance thermal losses.

$R _ { 0 } { = } R _ { 0 } \big ( S O C ( t ) , T ( t ) , S O H \big )$ This is a variable related to SOH.

This means that a portion of the electrical energy output by the battery is directlyconverted into heat within the battery itself, rather than being supplied to the phone. Thisexplains why the battery itself heats up significantly under heavy loads.

To determine whether the battery has reached its limit, we set $\Delta ( t )$ to assess itspower consumption state.

$$
\Delta (t) = V _ {o c} ^ {2} - 4 R _ {0} P _ {b a t t}
$$

When:

$\Delta \left( t \right) > 0$ :The battery can meet the load power demand by adjusting its output current,ensuring normal system operation.

$\Delta ( t ) { = } 0$ :The system has reached its maximum power transfer point. At this point, anyslight increase in load or voltage fluctuation will cause current to becomeuncontrollable.

$\Delta ( t ) < 0 : \mathrm { A }$ physical "voltage collapse" occurs. This typically happens when thebattery is extremely low on charge or in extremely cold environments, whereexcessive internal resistance prevents the battery from maintaining the minimumsystem operating voltage.

In the algorithm, once $\Delta \left( t \right) < 0$ the model immediately triggers the TTE termination signal,indicating a forced shutdown.

# 4.2.2 Time to First Termination. (non-regression)

This model defines endurance time not as a linear regression based on historical data, butas a first-time-to-reach (TTE) problem within the state space of a dynamic system. Wedeconstruct shutdown behavior as a physical-level "boundary game": When terminal voltagetouches the protection thresholdV cut, chemical energy SOC is depleted, or load demand exceedsthe battery's power output limit $( \varDelta ( t ) < 0 )$ , the system equilibrium breaks, triggering atermination signal. This modeling approach enables us to precisely capture nonlinear power-down behavior under extreme conditions, providing a scientifically grounded physicalexplanation for 'unpredictable depletion'.

Regarding the shutdown phenomenon, we have categorized it into three distinct triggeringcauses.

Voltage threshold trigger: $V _ { \mathrm { \it t e r m } } ( t ) \leq V _ { \mathrm { \it c u t } }$ ：

This is the most common cause of shutdown. To protect lithium batteries from over-discharge

Energy depletion trigger: $S O C \left( t \right) \le S O C _ { m i n }$

This represents ideal state depletion.

Power unfeasibility trigger (voltage collapse): $\Delta \left( t \right) < 0$ ：

This represents ideal state depletion.

This is the core explanation for "unexpected shutdowns." During extreme cold (whereinternal resistance $R _ { 0 }$ surges) or intensive gaming (where power $P _ { b a t t }$ peaks), thediscriminant suddenly drops below zero. At this point, the battery can no longerdeliver sufficient current to sustain the load—both mathematically and physically—resulting in a "voltage collapse." This is the model's key to explaining "why the screensuddenly blacks out with $1 0 \%$ battery remaining."

# 7 Sensitivity Analysis

模型的分析 ：在建模比赛中模型分析主要有两种，一个是灵敏度(性)分析，另一个是误差分析。灵敏度分析是研究与分析一个系统（或模型）的状态或输出变化对系

统参数或周围条件变化的敏感程度的方法。其通用的步骤是：控制其他参数不变的情况下，改变模型中某个重要参数的值，然后观察模型的结果的变化情况。误差分析是指分析模型中的误差来源，或者估算模型中存在的误差，一般用于预测问题或者数值计算类问题。

模型的检验：模型检验可以分为两种，一种是使用模型之前应该进行的检验，例如层次分析法中一致性检验，灰色预测中的准指数规律的检验，这部分内容应该放在模型的建立部分；另一种是使用了模型后对模型的结果进行检验，数模中最常见的是稳定性检验，实际上这里的稳定性检验和前面的灵敏度分析非常类似，等会大家看到例子就明白了。

在美赛的写作中，写的最多的就是灵敏度分析（Sensitivity Analysis），因此这里我们的标题就直接取得是灵敏度分析；如果你既要写灵敏度分析，又要写误差分析（Error Analysis），那么你可以把标题改成： Sensitivity Analysis and Error Analysis
